How to Solve a Rubik's Cube

Introduction

Have you ever picked up a Rubik's Cube and had no idea what to do with it? If so, here's a guide on how to master the 3x3 Rubik's Cube.

The Cube

- There are six colored sides: White, yellow, green, blue, red, and orange.

- Center pieces: Pieces with one color located in the center of each face.

- Edge Pieces: Pieces with two colors located in between the corner pieces.

- Corner Pieces: Pieces with three colors located at each corner of the cube.

Move Notations

Each algorithim uses standard notation to list each move required. Each move is represented using capital letters to tell which face to turn.

- R (right) -L (left)

-U (up/top) -D (down/bottom)

-F (front) -B (back)

- No Apostrophe: Turn the face clockwise

- Apostrophe ('): Turn the face counter-clockwise

- 2: Turn the face twice

Now that you know the cube and terminology, lets get started!

Step 1: The White Cross

1. Hold the cube so the white face is on the bottom and the yellow face is on the top.

2. Locate the white edge pieces and twist the faces around until all four white edges surround the yellow center piece, forming a "daisy".

3. Now, look at the corresponding color to one of the white edge pieces. Rotate the yellow face around until that color aligns with its matching center piece.

4. Twist the face with the two aligned colors 180 degrees, or two times.

5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 until you have a white cross on the bottom, with the edges aligned with the right centers.

Step 2: Solve the White Corners

1. Hold the cube with the white cross on the bottom.

2. Locate a corner piece in the top layer with a white tile and note the other two colors.

4. Hold the cube so that the corner piece is on your right hand side and repeat the algorithm, R U R' U', until the corner is properly solved in the bottom.

5. Repeat steps 2, 3, and 4 until the bottom layer is solved.

Note: If there is a corner piece stuck in the bottom layer, simply perform the same algorithm, R U R' U' to take it out.

Step 3: Solve the Middle Layer

1. Look at the top layer for an edge piece that does not contain yellow.

2. Rotate the top layer until the edge piece's front facing color is aligned with its matching center.

3. Now, look at the colors of the edge piece to determine whether to move the piece left or right.

4. To move the piece right: U R U' R' U' F' U F

5. To move the piece left: U' L' U L U F U' F'

Note: If an edge piece is in the wrong slot or not oriented correctly, use the same algorithm to take it out.

Step 4: The Yellow Cross

For this step, you need to know the cross algorithm: F R U R' U' F'

1. Still hold the cube with the white face at the bottom, and look at the yellow face. There should be either a dot, an L shape, a horizontal line, or a solved cross.

2. If there is a dot: Perform the cross algorithm and continue to step 3.

3. If there is an L shape: Hold cube so that one yellow edge faces away from you and one is to the left. Perform the cross algorithm and continue to step 4.

4. If there is a horizontal line: Hold cube so the three yellow tiles are horizontal to you. Perform the cross algorithm and you should now have a yellow cross.

Now, all yellow edges should be facing up and should form a yellow cross

Note: When looking for the dot, L shape, and horizontal line patterns, only look at the edge pieces and ignore the corners.

Step 5: Solve the Yellow Corners

For this step, you will need to know the Sune Algorithm: R U R' U R U2 R'

2. Repeat the Sune Algorithm until the top layer is completely yellow. Make sure reorient the cube after each algorithm to the correct state.

Step 6: Position the Top Corners

For this step you will need to know the Corner Swap Algorithm: R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

1. Look at the side of the top layer and try to find two corner pieces where they are on the same side and are the same color.

2. If you cannot find two matching corner pieces, simply perform the Corner Swap Algorithm and they will be there.

3. Once you have two matching corner pieces, hold the cube so that the matching corners are on the back face and facing opposite from you.

4. Perform the Corner Swap Algorithm. Now, all of the corners should be solved.

Step 7: Solve the Edges

1. Look to see if there is one side face completely solved. If so, have that face on the back face and facing opposite from you.

2. Look to see if the edge pieces need to be moved clockwise or counter-clockwise.

3. Perform the Edge Permutation Algorithm:

Algorithm (Clockwise): F2 U R' L F2 R L' U F2

Algorithm (Counterclockwise): F2 U' R' L F2 R L' U' F2

4. If there was no fully solved side, just perform the algorithm once and then complete steps 2 and 3.

Congratulations! If you have followed all of the steps correctly, you should now have a fully solved cube.

Now that you have mastered the beginner's method, keep practicing to improve your time, and you might be able to move on to more advanced methods like CFOP!
